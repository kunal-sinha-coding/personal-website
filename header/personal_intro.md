Welcome! I'm an ML engineer based in San Francisco. This blog is devoted to running cool experiments in model post-training.

I currently work at Fieldguide, building long horizon Agents for financial audit. My focus is twofold: designing high-quality evaluations, and optimizing the Agent harness to improve on these evaluations. Some projects include building a retrieval system, distilling searchable lessons from prior runs, and automating the prompt optimization loop.

In 2025, I completed my Bachelors and Masters at Stanford University. I did research with their NLP Group on topics such as neural graph retrieval, chain-of-thought reasoning, visual question answering, and computational linguistics.
